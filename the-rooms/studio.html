Zone 1 — The Piano

THE
PIANO

I never grew up playing instruments. This wasn't something I was taught. I just always had this vision — a grand piano at the bottom of a spiral staircase. So I started learning… from scratch. One day I'll have it all — might as well know how to play it.

Zone 2 — The Mic

THE
MIC

This is the same mic I started with. One of the first things I spent real money on. Back then, it felt like a risk — but I knew I had to take it seriously. I never replaced it. Didn't really feel the need to. Everything I've ever made started here. This is where the magic happens.

Zone 3 — Wavelength

WAVE
LENGTH

This is the actual licence plate from the first song and music video I ever released. It was one of the earliest songs I made where I just knew… this is the one. I even said it before it happened — this is going to get played on the radio. And it did. I keep it here as a reminder — I turned an idea into a dream come true… and I can do it again.
