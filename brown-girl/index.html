Adam Harris

Adam Harris is a British South Asian R&B artist of Pakistani and Bengali heritage, bringing contemporary R&B into a space shaped by his own identity and influences.

His new single “Brown Girl” puts that identity at the centre. Written as a celebration of brown women, the record blends contemporary R&B with a hook designed to feel immediately familiar, confident and celebratory — with British South Asian women at the heart of its audience.

With Brown Girl, Adam is building the campaign beyond the record itself, connecting the song with South Asian creators, culture and communities across the UK.
